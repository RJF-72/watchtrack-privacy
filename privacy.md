# WatchTrack Privacy Policy

**Operated by Redemption Road Music Group, Abilene, Texas, USA**
**Effective date: October 24, 2026**
**Support: support@watchtrack.app**

WatchTrack — TV Time Importer & Where to Watch is a browser extension that
moves your TV show library out of TV Time and into WatchTrack, so your shows,
watch history, ratings, and watchlist come with you — and then helps you track
what to watch next. This policy explains, in plain language, what information
the extension works with and what it does not do.

**The short version:** your library lives on your own device. The extension
imports the TV Time export file you provide and stores your watch history,
ratings, and watchlist locally in your browser. The only outside service the
extension talks to for show information is TMDB, and the only thing it sends
there is the show name you search for. If you buy WatchTrack Pro, your payment
is processed by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. We do not
sell your data.

## Data We Access, and Why

- **Your TV Time export file** — a CSV or JSON file that you export from
  TV Time and choose to import. It contains your shows, your watch history
  (the episodes you have marked as watched), your ratings, and your watchlist.
  The extension reads the file you provide, on your device, solely to recreate
  your library in WatchTrack. The extension never asks for, sees, or stores
  your TV Time password.
- **Your WatchTrack library** — the shows, watch history, ratings, and
  watchlist created by the import, plus anything you add or mark afterward, so
  the extension can show you your shows and keep your place.
- **Show names you search for** — when you search for a show, the extension
  sends that show name to TMDB (The Movie Database) to fetch public
  information about the show. See "Third Parties" below.

Your library information is never used to build a profile of you, to target
advertising, or for any purpose unrelated to importing and tracking your shows.

## Permissions, and Why Each One Exists

- **storage** — so the extension can save your imported watch history,
  ratings, and watchlist on your device using Chrome's extension storage.
- **Host permission** — used only to reach api.themoviedb.org (TMDB), so the
  extension can fetch public show metadata. The extension does not use host
  access for any other site.

## Where Your Data Is Stored

Your imported watch history, ratings, and watchlist are stored locally on your
device in your browser, using Chrome's built-in extension storage
(chrome.storage.local). They are not uploaded to any server operated by us,
and we cannot view them.

## Backups

WatchTrack can create local backups of your library — backup files stored on
your own device, under your control. As the listing says: local backup, your
data stays on your device. Restoring a backup reads a file you provide, the
same way the original import does.

## WatchTrack Pro and Payments (Stripe)

WatchTrack offers an optional Pro tier ($3.99/month or $15.99/year) that adds
unlimited tracking, automatic backups, and detailed statistics. Payments are
processed securely by **Stripe**, our payment processor. When you subscribe,
your payment details go directly to Stripe — the extension never receives,
sees, or stores your card number or other payment details, and the operator
receives no card data. Stripe's handling of your payment information is
governed by Stripe's own privacy policy. What we can see is limited to
ordinary subscription status information (such as whether a subscription is
active) needed to switch Pro features on or off.

## Third Parties

- **TMDB (The Movie Database)** — the extension fetches public show metadata
  from TMDB's API (api.themoviedb.org): things like show titles, episode
  lists, and descriptions. The only user-derived data sent to TMDB is the show
  name you type into search. These fetches are data-only — the responses are
  information, not code, and no code from TMDB is run in the extension. TMDB's
  use of the requests it receives is governed by TMDB's own privacy policy.
- **Stripe** — payment processing for WatchTrack Pro only, as described above.
- **TV Time** — a third-party service you already use, and the source of the
  export file you import. This extension is **not affiliated with or endorsed
  by TV Time**, and your use of TV Time remains governed by TV Time's own
  terms and privacy policy.

Other than TMDB and Stripe as described, the extension uses no third-party
services. It contains no analytics and no advertising.

## Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your information. We do not share your library, watch history,
ratings, or watchlist with anyone. We do not transfer your information to data
brokers, advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party.

## Data Retention

Because your library lives only in your browser's local storage, it stays
there until you remove it: it is deleted when you uninstall the extension, and
you can also erase it at any time by clearing the extension's data in Chrome's
settings. Local backup files you create stay on your device until you delete
them. We hold no copies of your library, so there is nothing on our side to
retain or delete. Stripe retains payment records under its own policy.

## Your Rights and Choices

- Importing is always your choice: nothing is read until you choose a TV Time
  export file and run the import.
- You can remove the extension at any time from Chrome's Extensions page;
  removing it deletes its locally stored data.
- You can clear the extension's stored library through Chrome's site/extension
  data controls without uninstalling.
- Questions about this policy or the extension's data practices:
  support@watchtrack.app.

## Children's Privacy

The extension is a general-purpose utility for moving and tracking a TV show
library. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly
collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address
with a new effective date. If a future version of the extension ever changed
how it handles information in a material way — for example, if any feature
ever sent library data off your device — that change would be described here
before or when it takes effect.

## Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

WatchTrack's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data
Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: information accessed through
Chrome's extension APIs is used only to provide the extension's single,
user-facing purpose — importing and tracking your TV show library — is not
transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide that feature
(TMDB for show metadata, Stripe for Pro payments, as described above), is not
used for advertising or credit-worthiness purposes, and is never read by a
human at Redemption Road Music Group.
